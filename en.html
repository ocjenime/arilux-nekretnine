06 · Off-Plan Purchase

Buy Today
at Yesterday's Price.

Buying off-plan is the smartest time to purchase: the lowest price, the widest selection of apartments, and a payment plan that follows the construction rhythm.

  1. 01

    Reservation

    You choose an apartment and reserve it with a 2,000 KM deposit. The apartment is yours and is taken off the market for 30 days.

  2. 02

    Preliminary Contract

    We sign a preliminary contract at the notary with a fixed price and payment plan. You pay 10% of the purchase price.

  3. 03

    Installments During Construction

    You pay the remainder in installments that follow the construction phases: no bank, no interest, no waiting.

  4. 04

    Technical Inspection

    Together we walk through the apartment before handover. We fix all defects at our own cost.

  5. 05

    Key Handover

    Property registration in the land registry and move-in. You'll receive a warranty for all completed work with your apartment.

Up to 15% Lower Price

The off-plan price is the lowest it will ever be. It rises with each completed construction phase.

You Choose the Finishes

Flooring, tiles, sanitary fittings, and wall colors: when buying off-plan, you choose, not us.

Value Rises Immediately

An off-plan apartment gains an average of 10 to 20% in market value by move-in.
